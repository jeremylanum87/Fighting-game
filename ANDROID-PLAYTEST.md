# Android acceptance check

Automated checks cover combat timing, guard rules, boss windup/recovery, motion-sheet dimensions, two simultaneous diagnostic pointers and cleanup. Native Canvas previews verify sprite framing and effects. A physical Android phone is not available in the workspace; the checks below remain to be performed on-device.

1. Open the game in Android Chrome. Use PHONE CHECK to hold MOVE TEST and ATTACK TEST together; confirm best simultaneous reaches 2 and returns to 0 active on release.
2. Play a complete Quick Match in portrait, then landscape. Confirm BLOCK and SUPER are reachable and all arena-footer buttons fit. Repeat with swapped control sides and with the thumbstick enabled.
3. Jump with Jade, attack with Brick, and use a super. Check that fighter heads, the HUD and attack tells remain readable. Rotate mid-fight; combat should pause and release held inputs.
4. Play several rounds with Auto effects. Return to PHONE CHECK to inspect recent FPS. If the game feels slow, try Low effects and repeat. Compare Full only if performance is comfortable.
5. In Training, practice light → heavy → special on Volt and Brick, then try all eight character routes. Confirm the visible windup, strike and recovery align with hit timing and coach feedback.
6. Reach The Promoter. Stand-block Hostile Takeover; low-block or jump Floor Tax; avoid Velvet Rope. Punish the recovery after a missed heavy. Confirm his portrait and black-and-gold outfit remain distinct through intro, damage, victory and super.
7. Finish a Quick Match and rematch. Confirm fighters, stage and difficulty stay selected and score/meters reset.

If a check fails, record phone model, Chrome version, orientation, control mode, effects mode and the action that failed.

8. Enable AUDIO, adjust Music and Effects / voice independently, and test the announcer toggle. Switch stages and listen for different grooves. Mute audio and background the browser; music and voice should stop.
9. Open DAILY CHALLENGES, complete an offered goal in Quick Match or Arcade, reload, and verify saved progress. Training should not count.
10. Load online until the title reports offline files ready, then use INSTALL / OFFLINE (or Chrome ⋮ → Install app). Close the old browser tab, enable airplane mode, and launch from the home screen. Test Quick Match, Training and character artwork. Browser voices may require an installed offline voice.

11. Complete LEARN TO PLAY using the buttons, then repeat movement/jump with the stick. Check that the lesson card, arena and controls fit together in landscape. Pick Midnight on fighter selection and confirm the outfit changes without affecting stats.
12. Win one arcade round, return to the title, close/reopen the app and use CONTINUE ARCADE. Confirm rival, difficulty and round score survive, while health and timer restart. Repeat after winning a whole match and after a loss.

13. In Match Setup select each new stage, including Cleveland / Cuyahoga Cypher. Verify the preview and fight background agree, fighters stand on the same ground line, and movement stays responsive with Full and Low effects. Try the five new stages offline after updating the app cache.

14. Enable AUDIO and wait for the combat pack status in Controls. Compare light/heavy/kick/block/throw/landing sounds, including rapid combos. Confirm sounds vary without harsh peaks, music softens during announcements, volume sliders stay independent, and mute/pause/background stops active sounds. Repeat offline after cache setup.

15. In Controls, enable the DJ announcer and try DJ VOICE → TEST DJ VOICE. Check saved voice selection after reload, music ducking during calls, and that mute/pause/background stops the speech cue. Test local voices offline if supported on the phone.

16. Start Survival, win a wave and verify health recovers by 18% of maximum and meter carries over. Lose, retry, and verify fresh health/meter. Reload and check best wave/score persist.
17. Play a Rival Set through three matches. Check set score persists between matches, round scores reset, and the displayed adaptation follows repeated lows/projectiles/throws/jumps.
18. Finish a match and inspect combo/punish/finisher highlights. Continue into the next match, or close to result options. Open Character Mastery, change fighters, reload to confirm saved XP, and check Master outfit unlocks at Headliner. Training must award no XP.

19. Try all three specials with each fighter. Check launch/impact colors remain readable, pause freezes the bursts, and Low FX reduces detail. Compare several rapid specials in Full and Auto effects on the phone.

20. Preview all eight fighters through stage entrance, taunt and victory. Check paired dialogue and that attacks still align with hitboxes. Test pause during celebrations, outfit colors, Low FX crowd lights and reduced-motion versus cards.

21. Tap a heavy near the end of a missed light attack: it should execute after recovery. Rapidly tap different attacks and verify only the newest pending action follows; pause/background must clear queued attacks and held touches.
22. Block melee attacks at each wall; the attacker should move back. After a full knockdown, test movement/jump or a buffered attack during the brief wake-up protection. Repeat as both fighters.
23. Compare early/late Arcade opponents at each difficulty. Check light/heavy hit recoil, rapid supers in Auto/Low FX, narrow-screen footer wrapping, rotation and simultaneous move/attack input. Record actual phone FPS using PHONE CHECK.
