# Accepted story choices

These user-approved choices supersede the earlier proposed Promoter-contract plot.

## Main story

- A hidden realm is breaking into the city, altering streets and allowing creatures to cross over.
- A respected city leader is an outsider from that realm, present for centuries and close to completing a takeover.
- The leader deliberately opened the passage.
- A few people develop rare abilities that can close the breach. Eliminating these defenders is necessary for the takeover.
- A legendary fighter discovered the threat and created a tournament to gather and prepare the defenders.
- The legend disappeared and is being corrupted within the hidden realm.
- At the climax, the legend briefly regains control and sacrifices their life to help seal the breach.
- The legend's final mixtape carries clues. Music is NOT the source of the supernatural threat or powers.

## Protagonist

- An ordinary neighborhood fighter enters for recognition.
- Loud, charismatic and funny; enjoys an audience and hides insecurity.
- Takes reckless risks for applause, sometimes endangering others.
- Uses improvised street fighting: borrowed techniques and unpredictable tricks.
- Awakens an ability to disrupt supernatural powers through strikes.
- Repeated use causes pain, exhaustion and possible collapse.
- Name, appearance, history and whether this is an existing roster fighter are undecided. Volt is a prototype playable slot, not an assumed identity for this protagonist.

## Rival

- First recognizes the disruption ability after experiencing it during a match.
- Wants to recruit the protagonist, believing their abilities together could be formidable.
- Their ultimate motivation is undecided. This is where the user paused lore development.
- Name, power, style and history remain undecided.

Do not fill open questions with old biographies as if they were approved canon.
