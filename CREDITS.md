Original sprite artwork is unchanged. Neon glow, ground reflections, shield effects, and animation integration are game rendering effects. The two attack buttons select the sprites' two sword attack animations; the underlying light/heavy damage values are retained.

## Combat mechanics
Adapted move frame data, motion recognition approach, input buffering, combo cancel routes, and damage scaling from Neon Fist by Rutu (developerrutu), MIT license.
Source: https://github.com/developerrutu/neon-fist (c28ffb7e7e81ed8f9cec274772c43c30d2876d42)
Full license: NEON-FIST-LICENSE.txt.

## Expanded roster
Cipher, Brick, Jade, Rook, Haze and Goldie use runtime color variants of the two CC0 LuizMelo animated sprite sets, with separate names and movement/damage tuning. They are not six additional original sprite packs.

## Street-rig animation update
The active fighters, title art and roster previews now use original procedural pixel-art streetwear rigs, with eight-frame special animation phases and original effects. The earlier LuizMelo assets remain in the repository with their CC0 credits, but the current rendering uses the new rigs.
